# Learning Pathways

Math Visual Trainer is organized around three top-level learning pathways. This keeps the product from becoming only a fast-answer drill tool and gives each learning goal a clear home.

## Visual Arithmetic

Visual Arithmetic is the core pathway for number sense. It focuses on visual quantity recognition, ten-frames, grouping, make-10 thinking, and early arithmetic concepts.

This pathway is separate because some children can memorize answers without understanding quantities. The MVP should preserve a place where children can learn visually before being asked to answer quickly.

## Arithmetic Fluency

Arithmetic Fluency is the practice and automation pathway. It is for children who already understand the concepts and need stronger accuracy, response stability, and gradually better speed.

This pathway keeps timed practice, tests, and challenges connected to mastery. Speed should not unlock harder work by itself; accuracy and stability remain the first signals.

## School Curriculum

School Curriculum is a future pathway. It will be organized around Slovak primary school curriculum cycles and parent-friendly grade navigation.

This is intentionally marked as coming soon in the MVP scaffold. The existing math engine and level model stay intact while the app gains a top-level structure that can later support school topics such as multi-digit addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and word problems.

Grade navigation should help parents understand where school topics live, but it should not hard-lock children. A child may need visual arithmetic remediation even when they are in a higher grade, or they may be ready for fluency work before a formal school cycle reaches that topic.
