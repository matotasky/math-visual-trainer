# Pedagogy

## Teaching Order

Math Visual Trainer follows this order:

1. Visual understanding
2. Mental strategy
3. Memorization
4. Speed after accuracy and stability

The app must not become a speed drill that rewards guessing. Timed work is introduced gradually and only after the child has enough conceptual stability.

## Diagnostic First

The child starts with diagnostic tasks. The app must not assume the child already knows addition to 10.

Diagnostic order:

1. Quantity recognition and number matching as a foundation check
2. Addition and subtraction to 10
3. Make-10 combinations
4. Bridge-through-10 examples
5. Addition and subtraction to 20
6. Whole tens to 100
7. Simple two-digit examples without regrouping

Low-number tasks remain available for diagnosis and remediation. They are not the default long path for an older child who already has the visual foundations.

## Level Path

The main MVP progression is designed for fluency training rather than grade-by-grade textbook coverage:

1. Addition and subtraction to 10
2. Bridge through 10
3. Addition and subtraction to 20
4. Whole tens to 100
5. Two-digit arithmetic without regrouping
6. Two-digit arithmetic with regrouping
7. Three-digit mental strategies
8. Mixed fluency

This keeps the app useful for a third-grade learner while still allowing the diagnostic to catch weak foundations.

## Visual Models

MVP visual models:

- Dots for small quantities
- Ten-frame for make-10 and addition to 10
- Number line for movement and comparison
- Groups for decomposing quantities
- None for later automatic recall tasks

Future visual models can include blocks or cubes.

## Mastery

Progression is based on:

- Accuracy
- Average response time
- Attempt count
- Correct streak
- Recent performance
- Mistake frequency
- Stability across sessions

One good session is not enough to unlock the next level.

## Mistake Interpretation

The app should distinguish:

- Lack of understanding
- Slow but correct thinking
- Fast guessing
- Repeated conceptual mistakes

Example: repeated errors on `8+2`, `7+3`, and `6+4` suggest weak make-10 understanding.

## Feedback Tone

Child feedback must be friendly, specific, and non-punitive. The UI should encourage retry and strategy use instead of shame or pressure.
